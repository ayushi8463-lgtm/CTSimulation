Dataset generator: controlled variations

Uses the objects, source, detector, scan and effects settings from the form on the left as the base scan, then changes the parameters you choose. Every combination is saved as a reconstruction-ready dataset, and all of them are bundled in one zip with a manifest. Material variations bundle material type, material name/formula, and density into one choice.
